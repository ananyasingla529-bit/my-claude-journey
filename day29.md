# Day 29 – Operation Lifeline: Supply Chain Crisis Lab

## 🚀 Challenge

Built **Operation Lifeline: Supply Chain Crisis Lab**, an interactive simulation game where users take the role of a Supply Chain Crisis Manager and make strategic business decisions during real-world disruption scenarios.

The application generates a unique company profile and crisis every time, allowing users to experience different supply chain challenges while improving their decision-making skills.

## ✨ Features

- Random fictional company generation
- Dynamic supply chain crisis scenarios
- Interactive War Room decision making
- Supplier negotiation simulator
- CEO Boardroom leadership quiz
- AI-powered business strategy selection
- Executive performance dashboard
- Animated progress bars & score tracking
- Personalized business recommendations
- Replay with new randomized scenarios

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- HTML5
- CSS3
- React (CDN)
- Babel JSX
- Vanilla JavaScript

## 📚 What I Learned

- Fundamentals of supply chain management
- Crisis response planning
- Leadership decision making
- Supplier negotiation strategies
- Business resilience concepts
- Building multi-step React applications
- State management using React Hooks
