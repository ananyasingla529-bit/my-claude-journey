## 📚 What I Learned
- A great product alone isn't enough; distribution and customer acquisition are equally important.
- Revenue models should be validated early instead of focusing only on features.
- Understanding customer acquisition costs and pricing strategy is essential for startup growth.
- Business Model Canvas helps visualize the complete business ecosystem.
- Investors evaluate execution capability, scalability, and market potential rather than just the idea.
