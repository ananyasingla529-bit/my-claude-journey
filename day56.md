## 💡 Key Learning

One of the most important lessons today was designing for reliability.

Instead of depending entirely on AI, CareerIQ automatically falls back to an offline analysis engine whenever AI is unavailable, ensuring users always receive meaningful feedback.

Building trustworthy software is just as important as building intelligent software.
