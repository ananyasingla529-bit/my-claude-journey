# 🚀 Day 25 – AI Shark Tank Simulator

## Overview

Today's challenge focused on simulating a Shark Tank investment pitch using AI.

I pitched **Career Compass AI**, an AI-powered career guidance platform, to four AI investors who evaluated the startup based on scalability, execution, customer value, profitability, and investment potential.

## Startup Idea

Career Compass AI helps students discover suitable career paths through AI-powered assessments, personalized learning roadmaps, skill gap analysis, resume assistance, and interview preparation.

## Key Learnings

- Investors focus on solving real customer problems.
- Revenue model and scalability are critical for startup success.
- Customer acquisition strategy is just as important as product development.
- A compelling pitch requires clarity, confidence, and evidence.
