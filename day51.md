# 🚀 Day 51/60 – Product Discovery & Sprint Planning | #60DaysOfClaude

Today was all about thinking like a **Product Manager** before becoming a developer.

Instead of jumping directly into coding, I focused on defining the problem, validating the idea, and planning a realistic MVP for my 10-day capstone project.

## 💡 Project Chosen
**CareerIQ – Smarter Job Decisions Start Here**

An AI-powered career assistant that helps job seekers analyze their resume against a job description before applying. The goal is to provide meaningful insights such as fit score, skill gaps, eligibility checks, evidence-based recommendations, and actionable next steps.

## 📌 What I Worked On Today
- Conducted product discovery
- Defined target users and problem statement
- Finalized MVP scope
- Prioritized features to avoid scope creep
- Planned the complete implementation roadmap
- Prepared the project for development and deployment

## 🎯 MVP Highlights
- Resume Upload (PDF/Text)
- Job Description Analysis
- AI-Powered Fit Score
- Skill Gap Detection
- Eligibility Checker
- Evidence Panel
- Apply Confidence Meter
- Recommended Next Action
- Offline Analysis Fallback
- Local Analysis History
- Job Comparison

## 📚 Key Takeaway
A successful product isn't built by adding every feature—it starts with solving one real problem exceptionally well. Careful planning today will make development much smoother over the next few days.

Looking forward to building and deploying **CareerIQ** as my portfolio capstone project! 🚀
