Artificial Intelligence is changing the way we learn, build, and innovate.

This challenge is my commitment to learning in public, sharing my progress, and becoming a better engineer one day at a time.

# 🛠 My Approach

Rather than rushing to build large projects, I'm focusing on **consistent improvement**.

For every challenge, I'll follow a simple process:

1. Understand the problem.
2. Research and explore possible solutions.
3. Design before building.
4. Develop clean and maintainable solutions.
5. Test and refine the project.
6. Document key learnings.
7. Share the journey publicly.

The goal isn't just to finish tasks—it's to understand *why* things work and become a better developer with every iteration.
