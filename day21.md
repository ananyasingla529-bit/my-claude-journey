# Day 21 – Digital Privacy Dashboard 🔐📊

## Objective

Built an interactive Digital Privacy Dashboard using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript that analyzes a user's digital footprint based on a sample dataset and presents privacy insights through modern dashboard visualizations.

---

## What I Learned

Today's project focused on transforming raw digital service usage into meaningful privacy analytics while clearly distinguishing between factual information and estimated insights.

- Digital services collectively reveal much more than we often realize.
- Privacy dashboards become more meaningful when facts and estimates are clearly separated.
- Good prompt engineering helps AI generate structured analytical dashboards.
- Interactive visualizations improve understanding of complex datasets.
