## 💡 Key Learning

One of the most important architectural decisions was designing both the AI engine and the offline fallback to return the exact same response structure.

This allows the frontend to work seamlessly regardless of whether the analysis comes from AI or the offline engine, making the application more reliable, maintainable, and user-friendly.
