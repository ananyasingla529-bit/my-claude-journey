## Key Learnings

* Assigning a role changes the AI's perspective.
* Different roles provide different advice for the same question.
* Role-Based Prompting helps generate more targeted and relevant responses.
* Giving AI context improves output quality.

## Reflection

Today I learned that AI responds differently based on the role assigned to it. A founder focuses on business value and initiative, while a developer focuses on technical skills and coding. This showed me how Role-Based Prompting can be used to get more useful answers depending on the situation.
